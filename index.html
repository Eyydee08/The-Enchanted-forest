Eco-Adventure Destination

Where Philippine Nature Meets Imagination

Verdant — The Enchanted Forest is an eco-adventure theme park located at the lush foothills of Mount Makiling in Laguna, Philippines.

The location is deeply meaningful: Mount Makiling is internationally renowned for its rich biodiversity, majestic rainforests, and the beloved myth of Maria Makiling, guardian diwata of the mountain. Verdant transforms these legendary elements into an unforgettable modern tourism destination while keeping conservation and nature stewardship at its very core.

Unlike traditional amusement parks built around mechanical steel coasters, Verdant offers an immersive forest journey where guests explore living attractions seamlessly woven into the natural canopy, adrenaline adventures, and Philippine mythical lore.
  • Natural Harmony: Attractions built directly within and around living forest canopies, ensuring zero habitat disruption.
  • Living Filipino Folklore: Interactive encounters with Philippine mythical creatures including diwatas, the tikbalang, kapre, and the radiant sarimanok.
  • Day-to-Night Transformation: From high-flying canopy ziplines by day to glowing fairy-light trails and night shows by twilight.

5 Signature Forest Attractions

Step into a world where thrilling eco-adventures seamlessly blend with timeless Philippine folklore and the whispering canopy of Mount Makiling.

Canopy Living

1. Giant Tree Village

A breathtaking collection of elevated treehouses connected by wooden sky-walkways and suspension bridges high above the forest floor. Visitors can explore winding canopy trails, enjoy high-perch dining, relax in hammock lounges, or book an overnight stay under the stars.

Day Walkways & Overnight Treehouse Stays
High Adventure

2. Forest Zipline

Soar like a raptor across an exhilarating, dual-cable zipline spanning the lush treetops. Experience an unparalleled bird’s-eye aerial view of Mount Makiling's legendary mountain silhouette, misty ravines, and the emerald expanse of the Laguna landscape.

Guided by certified safety eco-marshals
Night Wonder

3. Fairy-Light Trails

As twilight settles over Mount Makiling, the forest transforms into a luminous dreamscape. Wander along illuminated walking trails guided by thousands of warm fairy lights, hand-crafted bamboo lanterns, and nature-inspired glowing botanical art installations.

Opens at 6:00 PM every evening
Storytelling Cruise

4. Enchanted Boat Ride

Drift along a tranquil forest lake shrouded in gentle mountain mist. This gentle water journey integrates immersive audio storytelling, interactive light projections, sound effects, and living vignettes illustrating the folklore of Laguna and Maria Makiling's enchanted realm.

25-minute scenic guided boat tour
Folklore & Animatronics

5. Mythical Forest

An interactive cultural walkthrough featuring life-sized artistic installations and animatronic recreations of legendary Philippine mythical figures—including the benevolent Diwata, the noble Tikbalang guardian, the towering Kapre of the ancient balete, and the dazzling Sarimanok.

Educational & Folklore Discovery

Visitor Guide & Travel Information

Everything you need for an effortless, enchanting journey from Metro Manila or neighboring provinces to Mt. Makiling, Laguna.

Getting Here (Laguna)

  • By Car from Manila: Approx. 1.5 to 2 hours via SLEX, exit at Calamba, follow the national highway toward Los Baños / Bay, Laguna.
  • Public Transit: Buses bound for Sta. Cruz / Los Baños depart regularly from Cubao, Buendia, and Alabang.
  • Park Shuttle: Complimentary electric shuttles operate between Los Baños Junction and the Verdant Main Gate.
  • Parking: Spacious eco-friendly parking lot with EV charging stalls.

Park Passes & Hours

  • Operating Hours: Tuesday to Sunday, 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM (Closed Mondays for forest maintenance).
  • Day Explorer Pass: Access to Giant Tree Village walkways, Mythical Forest, and Nature Center.
  • Twilight & Night Pass: Fairy-Light Trails, Enchanted Boat Ride, and Night Forest Show (from 5:30 PM).
  • All-Access Adventure: Includes Forest Zipline & unlimited boat rides.

Forest Guidelines & Tips

  • Footwear: Wear comfortable walking sneakers or hiking shoes suitable for wooden suspension bridges and trails.
  • Eco-Pledge: Strictly Leave-No-Trace; single-use plastics are prohibited within park grounds. Bring reusable bottles.
  • Weather Prep: Mt. Makiling microclimate can bring brief refreshing showers; light rain jackets are recommended.
  • Treehouse Bookings: Overnight stays must be reserved at least 2 weeks in advance.
